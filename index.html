Groq and Anthropic route through the proxy and won't connect from an in-chat preview — deploy first (see README), then test on the real URL.

Which provider should I use?

Groq (default, open-source, free) — no credit card, roughly 30 requests/min and up to ~1,000–14,400 requests/day depending on the model. Llama 3.3 70B Versatile is the best balance of quota and quality for this app. Kimi K2 on Groq is the closest open-weight model to Claude Sonnet on reasoning/code benchmarks, but it has a smaller quota and is occasionally pulled from the free catalog — treat it as a step-up option, not the default.

Gemini has a free daily quota too (flash-lite stretches it furthest) but enabling billing removes the wall if you want this as a daily driver.

OpenAI / Anthropic are paid-only, no free tier — use them if you already have credits and want the strongest possible coaching quality.
